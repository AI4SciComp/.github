# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AI4SciComp a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive technical feedback, respect
for differing viewpoints, and responsibility for reproducible scientific
claims. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, discriminatory
language, publication of private information, and other conduct inappropriate
in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization owner through GitHub.
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communication that
violate this code. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
